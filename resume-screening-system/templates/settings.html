Algorithm & Transparent Match Score Architecture

The matching engine produces an exact, transparent composite percentage (0–100%) computed using the formula:

Match Score = (0.35 × Required Skills) + (0.15 × Preferred Skills) + (0.30 × TF-IDF Cosine Similarity) + (0.20 × Experience Match)
1. Required Skills Match (35%) (Matched Required Skills / Total Required Skills) × 100
2. Preferred Skills Match (15%) (Matched Preferred Skills / Total Preferred Skills) × 100
3. TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity (30%) Semantic N-gram (1,2) text similarity using scikit-learn
4. Experience Duration Match (20%) Min(100, (Extracted Experience Years / Required Min Years) × 100)
Evaluation Tiers:
80% – 100%: Strong Match 60% – 79%: Moderate Match Below 60%: Low Match
